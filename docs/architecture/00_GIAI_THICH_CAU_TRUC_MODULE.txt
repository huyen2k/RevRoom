REVROOM - GIAI THICH CAU TRUC FILE VA MODULE
============================================================

1. MUC TIEU TO CHUC

Du an duoc chia thanh ba tang:
- shared: cac thanh phan dung chung cho moi chien luoc.
- strategy-a, strategy-b, strategy-c: logic rieng cua tung chien luoc.
- UI: hien thi mo phong, ton kho, protection level, lich gia va Monte Carlo.

Moi chien luoc co the duoc doc, kiem thu va phat trien doc lap. Simulation tong hop chi lam nhiem vu dieu phoi, khong chua toan bo thuat toan cua A/B/C.

2. CAC FILE MODEL DUNG CHUNG

src/model/types.ts
- Dinh nghia Config, Customer, Day, Transaction, State, Policy.
- Dinh nghia tham so mac dinh, ten chien luoc va ham validate.
- Day la ket qua mot ngay cua mot chien luoc; State luu lich su 30 ngay cua A/B/C.

src/model/shared/customerGenerator.ts
- Sinh lich khach 30 ngay.
- Ho tro Poisson va Normal approximation.
- Tao WTP theo phan khuc, booking curve va thu tu khach den.
- Dung PRNG Mulberry32 de cung seed + cung cau hinh tao cung du lieu.
- CustomerGenerator co generate(), getSchedule(), allCustomers(), dailySummary().

src/model/demand.ts
- Cung cap remainingForecast() cho EMSR-b.
- Re-export API customer generator de giu tuong thich voi cac file cu.

src/model/shared/statistics.ts
- Tinh mean, median, sample standard deviation, min, max va CI 95% cua mean.
- quantile() tinh phan vi bang noi suy tuyen tinh.

src/model/shared/monteCarlo.ts
- Chay nhieu seed lien tiep.
- Trong moi seed, A/B/C dung chung mot tap khach (paired comparison).
- Tong hop thong ke tung chien luoc va so sanh hieu so theo cap.
- Xuat CSV chi tiet tung seed.

3. CHIEN LUOC A - FCFS

src/model/strategy-a/fcfs.ts
- simulateFcfsDay() xu ly mot ngay theo FCFS.
- Kiem tra WTP truoc, sau do kiem tra ton kho.
- Khach du kha nang chi tra duoc chap nhan neu con phong.
- Khong tu choi khach de danh phong cho phan khuc gia cao hon.
- Protection level luon bang 0.

4. CHIEN LUOC B - EMSR-b

src/model/strategy-b/emsrB.ts
- poissonQuantile(), normalQuantile(), demandQuantile(): tinh phan vi nhu cau.
- emsrB(): tinh hai protection level long nhau tu cac lop gia cao hon.
- Mang gia va nhu cau duoc sap xep tu thap den cao.

src/model/strategy-b/simulation.ts
- simulateEmsrDay(): mo phong mot ngay voi gia co dinh va protection level.
- Khach phan khuc gia thap co the bi tu choi de bao ve ton kho cho phan khuc gia cao.

5. CHIEN LUOC C - DYNAMIC PRICING + EMSR-b

src/model/strategy-c/dynamicPricing.ts
- calculateDynamicPrices(): cap nhat he so va ba muc gia tai dau moi ngay.
- Gia duoc gioi han boi min/max va lam tron den 10.000 dong.

src/model/strategy-c/simulation.ts
- simulateDynamicEmsrDay(): tinh gia dong truoc, sau do tinh lai EMSR-b.
- Gia giu nguyen trong ca ngay; ngay sau moi cap nhat.

6. DIEU PHOI VA GIAO DIEN

src/model/simulation.ts
- initial(): tao State rong.
- policyDay(): chuyen mot ngay den module A/B/C tuong ung.
- step(): cap nhat mot ngay cho ca ba chien luoc.
- run(): chay tron 30 ngay.

src/model/metrics.ts
- KPI mot ngay, CSV ket qua mot lan mo phong va API Monte Carlo tuong thich.

src/App.tsx
- Giao dien React: dashboard, animation khach, ton kho, Monte Carlo.
- Hien thi protection level cua B/C theo ngay.
- Bang lich gia dong va bang giao dich.

src/Method.tsx
- Noi dung giai thich mo hinh trong giao dien.

src/style.css
- Giao dien va responsive layout.

6. TEST

tests/model.test.ts: kiem thu tong hop, seed, toan ven giao dich, EMSR-b, dynamic pricing, CSV va Monte Carlo.
tests/strategy-a/fcfs.test.ts: kiem thu FCFS doc lap.
tests/strategy-b/emsr-b.test.ts: kiem thu EMSR-b va mo phong B.
tests/strategy-c/dynamic-pricing.test.ts: kiem thu tinh gia va mo phong C.

tests/ui.spec.ts: kiem thu giao dien bang Playwright (neu cau hinh browser da duoc cai dat).

7. CAC FILE TUONG THICH

src/model/customerGenerator.ts va src/model/emsr.ts la cac entry point re-export de giu import path cu. Code chinh nam trong shared/customerGenerator.ts va strategy-b/emsrB.ts.

8. LUONG DU LIEU

Config + Seed
  -> CustomerGenerator
  -> CustomerSchedule 30 ngay
  -> Strategy A / Strategy B / Strategy C (moi chien luoc co kho 100 phong rieng)
  -> State / histories
  -> KPI, chart, room grid, transaction log, CSV

Monte Carlo lap lai luong tren voi nhieu seed. Trong moi seed, A/B/C nhan cung mot CustomerSchedule. Day la thiet ke paired experiment de giam nhieu do khac biet giua cac tap khach.
